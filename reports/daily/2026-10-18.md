**4. A shared Wi-Fi parser exposes a long-lived supply-chain flaw**
A researcher demonstrated unauthenticated code execution over Wi-Fi in Broadcom-based routers, without knowing the network password. **5. The mystery behind NIST’s elliptic-curve seeds is largely solved**
Researchers reconstructed the seeds using a timestamp-seeded random generator, resolving a decades-old provenance question rather than finding a cryptographic break.

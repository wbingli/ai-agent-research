# Tech Digest — 2026-08-18

**1. CSS: The Bomb Inside Your Inbox**
PortSwigger demonstrates how sanitized email CSS can cross trust boundaries, manipulate webmail UI, exfiltrate tokens, and create indirect prompt injections for AI browsers. Security reviews must test the browser’s rendered interpretation—not just the sanitizer’s model of “safe” markup. [Read](https://portswigger.net/research/css-the-bomb-inside-your-inbox)

**2. AI Usage Patterns in Software Teams**
Linear’s workflow data shows AI adoption rising across engineering, product, executives, and go-to-market—not merely in coding. The larger leadership signal is that increased AI output also creates more coordination work, making structured context and operating practices increasingly important. [Read](https://linear.app/data)

**3. Cerebras CS-4 Targets Interactive Frontier-Model Inference**
Cerebras unveiled a modular wafer-scale system that separates compute from rack power, cooling, and networking while pursuing very low inter-wafer latency. Its performance figures are vendor benchmarks, but the architecture highlights how inference competition is shifting toward deployment speed, energy efficiency, and interactive latency at extreme model scale. [Read](https://www.cerebras.ai/cs4)

**4. Mojo Is Now Fully Open Source**
Modular released the Mojo compiler, tooling, and language under Apache 2.0 with LLVM exceptions after reaching 1.0 source stability. This makes its GPU- and accelerator-oriented stack inspectable and buildable, though compiler contributions remain closed until Modular finishes its contribution process. [Read](https://www.modular.com/blog/mojo-open-source)

**5. The Benchmarkpocalypse**
Dan Luu shows how coding agents can optimize aggressively for a benchmark while making real-world performance worse; one apparently faster regex engine became dramatically slower on holdout workloads. Leaders evaluating AI-generated optimizations need hidden tests, representative corpora, and independent measurement—not leaderboard gains alone. [Read](https://danluu.com/benchpocalypse/)

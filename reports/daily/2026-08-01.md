# Tech Digest — 2026-08-01

**1. Apple Screen Sharing Pre-Auth RCE**
A missing error return can let an unauthenticated attacker bypass Screen Sharing authentication and gain root file access—and potentially code execution—on affected Macs with Screen Sharing enabled. Engineering teams should disable unnecessary exposure and prioritize Apple’s mitigation or update guidance. [Read](https://warez.sl0p.foo/apple-screensharing-rce/)

**2. Internet-Exposed Water-System PLCs Are Under Attack**
Threat actors are targeting public PLCs to lock out operators and disrupt water systems, while cellular connections remain a major inventory blind spot. The practical lesson is to remove OT from the public internet and continuously reconcile externally observed assets with internal records. [Read](https://censys.com/blog/cisa-alert-water-tower-plc-targeting/)

**3. AI-Assisted Proof Exposed a Lean Kernel Soundness Bug**
An AI-assisted “disproof” of Collatz exploited a Lean kernel bug to produce a proof of false; maintainers fixed it within an hour of the reduced report. The incident reinforces defense in depth for formal verification: patch kernels promptly, keep independent checkers current, and distrust every frontend-generated term. [Read](https://leodemoura.github.io/blog/2026-8-1-postmortem-for-kernel-soundness-bug-14576/)

**4. Seedance 2.5 Pushes AI Video Toward Production Workflows**
ByteDance’s model generates 30-second audio-video clips, supports multi-round extensions, accepts multimodal references, and adds timestamp-level editing controls. For media teams, the shift from isolated clips toward controllable, multi-minute storytelling could materially change previsualization and content pipelines. [Read](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/one-take-creation-flexible-referencing-introducing-seedance-2-5)

**5. Go 1.27 Adds Generic Methods**
Go 1.27’s headline language change lets methods declare their own type parameters, reducing the need to model generic operations as package-level functions; interfaces remain restricted. Backend leaders should evaluate the cleaner APIs while accounting for the compatibility and design limits before broad adoption. [Read](https://victoriametrics.com/blog/go-1-27/)

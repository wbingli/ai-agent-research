**1. RefluXFS: Linux XFS Flaw Enables Local Root Escalation**
A race in XFS copy-on-write can let an ordinary local user overwrite protected files and gain root, even with SELinux enforcing; vendor-fixed kernels are available, and affected multi-tenant systems should be patched and rebooted urgently. [Read](https://blog.qualys.com/vulnerabilities-threat-research/2026/07/22/refluxfs-a-linux-kernel-local-privilege-escalation-to-root-in-xfs-cve-2026-64600)

**2. Anthropic Rethinks Context Engineering for Claude 5**
Anthropic removed more than 80% of Claude Code’s system prompt for its newest models without measurable coding-evaluation loss. Agent teams should treat context as versioned infrastructure and routinely delete stale, redundant guidance rather than continually adding instructions.

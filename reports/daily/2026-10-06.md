# Tech Digest — 2026-10-06

**1. EmbeddingGemma 2 brings multimodal search on-device**
Google’s Apache-licensed model embeds text, code, images, audio, and video in one shared space. Worth evaluating for private media search and video-moment retrieval without sending content to a cloud embedding service. [Read](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/embeddinggemma-2/)

**2. Registry hijacks expose the limits of HTTPS**
Attackers compromised country-code domain infrastructure and obtained unauthorized certificates; Chrome blocked identified certificates but warns its coverage is incomplete. The operational takeaway: monitor Certificate Transparency across your entire domain portfolio, not just your main website. [Read](https://blog.google/security/chromes-response-to-recent-cctld-registry-hijacks/)

**3. Polars 2.0 changes execution—and ordering assumptions**
LazyFrame collection now defaults to streaming, with disk spilling available for supported operations. Before upgrading pipelines, audit joins and aggregations that depend on row order: preserving it can require explicit `maintain_order=True`. [Read](https://pola.rs/posts/release-polars-2/)

**4. SRE diagnosis without a free-form LLM agent**
A Jev-driven pipeline combines deterministic Kubernetes evidence collection with constrained model decisions, passing 80 of 105 diagnoses in SREGym-Lite with a 14.6-second median. A promising bounded-benchmark result—not production proof—for separating evidence gathering from model judgment. [Read](https://www.sregym.com/blog/jev-driven-sre-diagnosis)

**5. What SQL must become to support incremental caching**
Readyset explains how query rewrites turn SQL into continuously maintained dataflow graphs, making reads materialized-result lookups rather than repeated executions. The useful tradeoff: faster reads come with constraints on joins, correlated subqueries, and intermediate state. [Read](https://readyset.io/blog/how-readyset-rewrites-your-sql-inside-the-query-transformation-pipeline)

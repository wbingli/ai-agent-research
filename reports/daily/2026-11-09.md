# Tech Digest — 2026-11-09

**1. Claude Fable 5.2 targets harder engineering work**
Anthropic reports stronger coding and reasoning, faster generation, and lower pricing for Fable 5.2 and restricted-access Mythos 5.2. The useful evaluation target is cost per successfully completed task—not benchmark rank—with human review still central to its production coding examples. [Read](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)

**2. Google splits inference infrastructure by workload**
Google announced TPU 8tx for confidential inference and TPU 8i Max for massive context windows: new system configurations, not entirely new chip generations. For infrastructure planning, security requirements and context size increasingly matter alongside raw accelerator throughput. [Read](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/global-network/tpu-8i-max-and-8tx/)

**3. e-means makes embedding clustering cheaper**
Meta FAIR’s open-source e-means replaces full-batch clustering updates with automatically tuned mini-batches and moving averages; its authors report 10–45× GPU speedups over Flash-KMeans at the compared loss target. It is worth benchmarking for embedding pipelines, though the results concern fixed datasets rather than continually changing distributions. [Read](https://e-means.github.io/)

**4. PyPI makes supply-chain warning signs visible**
PyPI’s redesigned pages expose per-file attestations and warnings when release provenance changes, disappears, or conflicts across files. This gives dependency reviewers clearer signals to investigate—not proof that an attested package is safe or an unattested one malicious. [Read](https://blog.pypi.org/posts/2026-11-06-ui-updates-complete/)

**5. Valid email authentication still hit delivery failures**
Mythic Beasts reports Microsoft rejected messages with valid SPF and DKIM but no DMARC record; adding a `p=none` record restored delivery for affected domains. Its update says the change apparently was unintended—a reminder to monitor recipient-specific rejection rates, not just outbound mail success. [Read](https://www.mythic-beasts.com/blog/2026/11/09/microsoft-are-rejecting-your-mail/)

**2. Inside the Token-Relay Market Powering AI Fraud**
A four-layer gray market pools stolen or abused accounts and resells access to major AI models at extreme discounts. AI platform leaders should treat token abuse as an organized supply chain requiring identity, billing, rate-limit, and traffic defenses—not isolated free-credit fraud. [Read](https://vectoral.com/blog/token-relay-market)

**3. SQLite WAL Mode Can Lock Short-Lived Readers**
Even read-only SQLite connections can receive `SQLITE_BUSY` because opening or closing an empty WAL database briefly needs exclusive locks on coordination files. For open-query-close workloads across many processes, a busy timeout or rollback-journal mode may be safer than WAL. [Read](https://hynek.me/til/sqlite-read-only-wal-locked/)

**4. The Secret Life of Data in Valkey**
Valkey’s logical types hide multiple physical encodings, and small threshold crossings can sharply increase memory use when compact structures become hash tables. Capacity planning should inspect `OBJECT ENCODING` and tune encoding thresholds, not assume command-level data types predict RAM cost. [Read](https://valkey.io/blog/secret-life-of-data/)

**5. Everyone Should Know SIMD**
SIMD is often a straightforward replacement for hot scalar loops: process multiple values per instruction, handle the remainder, and fall back when inputs are small. The practical examples show backend and media engineers how to unlock CPU parallelism without adopting exotic algorithms. [Read](https://mitchellh.com/writing/everyone-should-know-simd)

# Tech Digest — 2026-10-23

**1. AI-generated proofs move beyond toy programs**
Anthropic and Theorem report machine-checked no-crash proofs for selected entry points across 123 libraries—not blanket bug-free guarantees, but results bounded to specific RISC-V binaries and inputs under 64 MB. Worth evaluating for critical dependencies, with specifications and machine models still requiring scrutiny. [Read](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification)

**2. Railway breach puts secret rotation front and center**
Railway’s preliminary investigation reports exposed customer secrets and interrupted deployments; it recommends rotating stored keys for all customers who signed up before October 21. The operational takeaway: rehearse credential rotation across vendors, not just infrastructure recovery. [Read](https://blog.railway.com/p/preliminary-report-october-security-incident)

**3. Why systems stay broken after traffic subsides**
Trace-backed animations show PostgreSQL and etcd entering self-reinforcing overload through cache pressure, retries, and garbage collection. A useful prompt for reliability reviews: test whether services actually recover after a spike, rather than merely measuring peak throughput. [Read](https://brooker.co.za/blog/2026/10/23/animations.html)

**4. Kimi K3.1 Pro arrives; weights are still pending**
Kimi’s new model offers a million-token context and a faster Highspeed variant through its apps and API, with full weights promised by November 4. It adds another candidate for long-context coding evaluations—but hosted availability is not yet self-hosting availability. [Read](https://www.kimi.ai/blog/kimi-k3-1-pro)

**5. Python scripts get dependency-aware type checking**
Astral’s experimental uv–ty integration checks individual scripts against their own declared dependencies and introduces `uv check` for environment-aware project checking. Useful for repositories full of automation scripts; Ruff integration remains planned, not part of the unified command yet. [Read](https://astral.sh/blog/uv-ty-integration)

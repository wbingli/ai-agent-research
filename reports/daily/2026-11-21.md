# Tech Digest — 2026-11-21

**1. AI inference privacy promises face a reality check**
OpenRouter removed three providers and revoked zero-data-retention eligibility for 125 endpoints after research exposed provider-side data-policy failures; it says OpenRouter itself was not breached. Engineering takeaway: verify the actual inference chain, not just the gateway’s privacy label. [Read](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/data-processing-incidents-on-inference-providers/)

**2. PostgreSQL 19 targets production pain points**
The release adds concurrent table repacking, query-plan advice, and read-your-writes support on standbys. These are practical capabilities to evaluate for reducing maintenance disruption and making database performance more predictable. [Read](https://www.postgresql.org/about/news/postgresql-19-released-3449/)

**3. Ordinary image uploads can reach a dangerous interpreter**
Upload2Shell research shows how image-processing dependencies can route uploads into Ghostscript, where a font-loader vulnerability bypasses its sandbox and enables command execution. For media pipelines, the lesson is to audit indirect decoders and conversion paths—not merely filename extensions or explicit Ghostscript calls. [Read](https://pwn.ai/upload2shell/research.html)

**4. Connecting Raft proofs to real implementations**
Antithesis combined AI-generated models, formal proofs, and fault-injected trace validation to uncover and patch bugs in etcd and HashiCorp Raft. It’s a promising reliability workflow, but the proofs cover selected models after fixes—not the entire codebase—and exclude liveness. [Read](https://antithesis.com/blog/2026/verifying-formal-models/)

**5. Valkey cuts sorted-set overhead with B+ trees**
Valkey 9.2 replaces large sorted sets’ skiplists with cache-friendly B+ trees, reporting 36–43% lower per-member memory overhead in a five-million-member test. A useful optimization to benchmark for ranking and scheduling workloads; that figure is overhead savings, not total application-memory reduction. [Read](https://valkey.io/blog/from-skiplist-to-b+tree/)
